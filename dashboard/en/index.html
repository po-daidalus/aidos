What is behind a Google rating?

Since April 2026, Google shows on German profiles how many reviews were removed following defamation complaints – tucked away in the reviews tab. aidos calculates how much that lifts the displayed rating.

The data basis

what is included — and what is not yet

aidos surveys city by city. Every known business with a removal notice is re-checked each month; the full pass through a city in search of new cases rotates between cities. This overview honestly shows which cities are already in the dataset — there simply are no figures from cities not yet surveyed.
